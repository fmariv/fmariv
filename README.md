I work at the intersection of geospatial technology, product development and project leadership, turning complex scientific and technical capabilities into products that solve real-world problems.

I'm currently **Head of Product at [EarthPulse](https://earthpulse.ai/)**, where I lead SPAI, our flagship geo-intelligence platform, and the Pulses product portfolio. My work combines product strategy, discovery, roadmap ownership and coordination of multidisciplinary teams to bring Earth Observation and AI solutions into operational use.

My background is in environmental science, geospatial engineering and data science. I've worked across the technology stack, from geospatial data processing and machine learning to APIs, cloud infrastructure and interactive mapping applications. Although my focus today is on leading products and projects, I still enjoy getting hands-on with technology.

## What I work on

- **Product & project leadership** — Product discovery, strategy, roadmaps, stakeholder management and technical project delivery.
- **Earth Observation & Geospatial AI** — Copernicus, satellite analytics, environmental intelligence and geospatial applications.
- **Data & engineering** — Python, FastAPI, Svelte, PostGIS, machine learning, cloud infrastructure and geospatial data pipelines.

## Beyond the code

I'm involved in the geospatial open-source community, including [QGIS España](https://www.qgis.es/) and [OSGeo](https://www.osgeo.org/). I also write **Beyond the Map**, a newsletter about product thinking, Earth Observation and turning data into decisions.
